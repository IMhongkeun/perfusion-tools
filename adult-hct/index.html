Current circulating volume is estimated as EBV + initial priming + entered net I/O change. This model assumes ideal mixing, no loss of red cells through HF/UF, and no unrecorded fluid shifts, bleeding or transfusion. For clinical decisions, rely on measured Hct and actual circuit volumes.

Formula and limitations

Pre-CPB: Hct (%) = [EBV × pre-Hct fraction + substituted RBC mL × RBC product Hct fraction] / (EBV + fixed priming mL) × 100. The RBC displaces an equal volume of crystalloid.

On-pump: Hct (%) = [estimated current volume × measured Hct fraction + added RBC mL × RBC product Hct fraction] / estimated current volume × 100. Matched HF/UF removes water, not red cells.

Simplified mass-balance estimates are sensitive to initial EBV and actual volume. They do not define transfusion thresholds, oxygen delivery, or the safety of a UF rate.
